FieldCraft external NGSolve runtime

FieldCraft runs the private Python interpreter as a separate process. The original upstream binary distributions provide CPython, NumPy, NGSolve, Netgen and the Open CASCADE dependencies. runtime.lock records the exact download URLs and SHA-256 digests. The private Python distribution keeps lib/python3.13/LICENSE.txt, and each wheel retains its distribution metadata and bundled legal notices under python/lib/python3.13/site-packages. For example, ngsolve and netgen-mesher include licenses/LICENSE, and netgen-occt includes licenses/LICENSE_LGPL_21.txt.
